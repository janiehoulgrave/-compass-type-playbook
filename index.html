Compass agent brand guide · Typography

Your fonts are part of your brand.

Your logo is a small mark in the corner. Your fonts are everything else on the page: every headline, price and detail on every flyer, post, postcard and sign. Used the same way every time, they make your marketing recognizable at a glance. This guide shows you why that matters and builds a type style guide made for you.

It goes on your personal style guide and every example in this guide.
