What is letter tracking?

Tracking is the even space between every letter in a word or line. More tracking spreads letters apart; less pulls them together. In the Compass platform you set it in steps of 10.

Same words, six personalities

The same two words can feel masculine or feminine, loud or quiet, classic or modern. Four simple choices make the difference:

CapitalizationALL CAPS feels formal and strong. Sentence case feels friendly.
ItalicsItalics lean and flow like handwriting, so they feel soft and personal.
WeightBold feels confident. Light feels calm and refined.
TrackingMore space between capitals feels airy and expensive.
